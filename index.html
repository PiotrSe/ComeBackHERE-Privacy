Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

ComeBackHERE is a mobile application that helps users remember and find the location where they parked their vehicle.

1. Information collected by ComeBackHERE

ComeBackHERE uses the device's location services to determine and save the user's parking location.

The parking location is used to provide the application's main functionality — helping the user find their parked vehicle.

ComeBackHERE does not require users to create an account or provide their name, phone number, or other personal information to use the core functionality of the app.

2. Location data

The application may request access to the device's location. Location data is used to determine the user's parking position and provide directions or information that helps the user return to that location.

ComeBackHERE does not sell the user's location data.

3. Advertising

ComeBackHERE uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and information about interactions with advertisements.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

More information is available in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data sharing

ComeBackHERE does not sell personal information.

The application uses third-party services, including Google AdMob for advertising. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data security

ComeBackHERE does not operate its own user account system or database for storing user accounts.

6. Children's privacy

ComeBackHERE is not specifically directed at children.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the ComeBackHERE application, please use the developer contact information provided on the ComeBackHERE Google Play Store listing.
