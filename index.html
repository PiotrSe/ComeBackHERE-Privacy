Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ComeBackHERE handles information when you use the ComeBackHERE mobile application.

1. Developer and Privacy Contact

ComeBackHERE is developed by the developer identified on the application's Google Play listing.

Privacy contact: devtrollo@gmail.com

2. Location Information

ComeBackHERE uses the location services of your Android device to determine where you parked your vehicle and to provide the application's parking-location and return functionality.

Location permission is requested only when it is needed for location-based features. You can control location permission through the Android settings on your device.

ComeBackHERE does not sell your location information.

3. Parking Location Storage and Deletion

The parking location used by ComeBackHERE is stored for the purpose of providing the application's parking-location functionality. The developer does not operate a user account system for storing parking locations.

The saved parking location can be replaced or removed through the application's functionality. Uninstalling the application also removes application data that is stored locally on the device, subject to the Android operating system and device backup settings.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

ComeBackHERE uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Depending on the device and advertising configuration, this may include:

  • IP address;
  • advertising and other device identifiers;
  • information about interactions with advertisements and the application;
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted during transmission. Google may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services and to help prevent fraud.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available in the Google Privacy Policy .

5. Sharing of Information

ComeBackHERE does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the application, including Google AdMob for advertising. Such providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Data Security

ComeBackHERE does not operate its own user account database or require users to create an account.

Information transmitted by third-party services used by the application is handled according to the security practices of those services. Google states that data collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

ComeBackHERE retains the saved parking location only for as long as it is needed to provide the parking-location functionality. The user can replace or remove the saved parking location using the application.

Advertising-related data may be retained and processed by Google according to Google's applicable policies and retention practices.

8. Children's Privacy

ComeBackHERE is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children as a separate user account or registration process.

9. Your Choices

You can control the application's access to device location through Android permission settings. You can also manage advertising-related settings, including the Android advertising ID, through the settings available on your Android device.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.
